This record exists so every shipped visual asset has a known origin and can be audited before release.

| --- | --- | --- |
| `src/main/resources/assets/redstonelabworks/icon.png` | Generated specifically for this project with OpenAI image generation on 27 August 2026; no reference image supplied; manually reviewed by the project maintainer | Project asset under MPL-2.0 to the extent copyright or related rights apply; subject to the applicable OpenAI terms |

The design prompts and source intentionally exclude Minecraft textures, blocks, item sprites, fonts, logos, and other Mojang/Microsoft branding. No Mojang asset is intentionally redistributed.

Contributors must add provenance for every new binary or visual asset. Do not commit an asset copied or traced from Minecraft, another mod, a website, or a third-party pack unless its license clearly permits the intended use and redistribution.
